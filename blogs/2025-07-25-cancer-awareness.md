CANCER AWARENESS: EARLY DETECTION AND TREATMENT OPTIONS IN NIGERIA Introduction Cancer is often spoken of in hushed tones in Nigeria, yet it remains a significant and growing health issue. Increased awareness, early detection, and accessible treatment can dramatically improve survival rates. This article explores how Nigerians can recognize cancer early and seek timely, effective care.
