Respiratory health is often only taken seriously when breathing becomes difficult. Yet conditions like asthma, allergies, and respiratory infections affect millions of people every year, often starting with symptoms that feel mild or easy to ignore. Asthma is one of the most common chronic respiratory conditions globally.
